## 👋 About Me

I'm a **Research & Development Engineer** with a background in **Mechatronics Engineering** and hands-on experience across robotics, control systems, machine learning, industrial automation, and embedded systems.

My work bridges **intelligent algorithms and physical systems**—from computer vision and robot kinematics to FPGA-based digital designs, microcontrollers, and PLC-driven automation. I enjoy researching, prototyping, reverse-engineering, and integrating practical engineering solutions.

Since **January 2021**, I have worked as an R&D Engineer at the **Higher Authority for Science, Technology, and Innovation (HASTI), Yemen**.
